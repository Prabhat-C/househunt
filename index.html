💻 On a laptop

W A S D or arrows to walk, Shift to run, mouse to look around. Click the game to capture the mouse; Esc lets it go.

📱 On a phone or tablet

Left thumb walks, right thumb looks around, and on-screen buttons do the rest. Turn the phone sideways for a bigger view.
Press 🎮 in the top-right corner of any game to come back here. The 3D library loads from the internet the first time.
